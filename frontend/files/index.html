About

Method

Two Random Forest models : a classifier that labels a sample LOW or HIGH risk, and a regressor that estimates concentration in mg/kg. The estimate is shown only when the sample is classed as high risk.

Data limits

Results depend on the training data: its location, season, species and sampling methods. Samples unlike the training data can give unreliable output. Small or uneven classes can also hide weak spots.

Estimates are not lab results

Model output is an estimate, not a laboratory measurement. It must not be used alone for food safety, harvest or public health decisions. Confirm with approved laboratory testing.
